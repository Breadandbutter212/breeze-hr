Privacy & data
How Breeze HR processes and protects your data.
UK GDPR notice: The company using Breeze HR is the data controller for employee data processed through this platform. Breeze HR acts as a data processor. Configure your data retention period under Settings.
What data Breeze HR processes

When you use Breeze HR, the following data is processed:

  • HR conversations - chat messages, questions and AI responses
  • Company documents - HR policies, handbooks and templates you upload
  • Employee data - names, job titles, salaries and other personal details entered when generating letters and contracts
  • Meeting notes - transcripts and written notes from recorded meetings
  • Email content - if you connect Gmail or Outlook, email content you view and draft responses to
  • Writing samples and style preferences - text pasted into Settings to train the writing style
  • Usage signals - ratings, edited drafts and conversation patterns used to improve response quality
Sub-processors

Breeze HR uses the following third-party sub-processors. Data may be transferred to servers outside the UK/EEA.

Lawful basis for processing

Processing of employee personal data through Breeze HR is carried out under the legitimate interests of the employer in managing its HR function efficiently, or under contractual necessity where applicable. Special category data (health, disciplinary matters) should only be entered where a specific lawful basis under UK GDPR Article 9 applies.

Meeting recordings

When using the meeting recorder, you are responsible for ensuring that all participants are aware the conversation will be transcribed and processed by AI. Under UK GDPR and the Investigatory Powers Act 2016, recording individuals without their knowledge may be unlawful. Always obtain consent or inform participants before recording.

Data retention

Your account administrator can configure how long data is retained under Settings - Data & privacy. After the retention period, chat history, meeting notes and response data are automatically deleted. Uploaded policies and templates are kept until manually deleted. You can also delete all company data immediately from Settings.

Your rights under UK GDPR

As a data controller, your organisation is responsible for handling data subject rights requests from employees. Breeze HR provides tools to delete all stored data on request. Rights include: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and the right to object.

FAQs
Everything you need to know about Breeze HR.
The platform
How is Breeze HR different from using ChatGPT or a general AI tool?
Most AI tools give you a generic answer based on what you typed. Breeze HR builds a picture of how your specific team works and gets more accurate the longer you use it.

The key differences:

It knows your business. Upload your HR policies and handbook and Breeze HR reads them and references them first when answering questions - so every response reflects your actual rules, not generic guidance. If your policy says something different to ACAS, Breeze HR follows your policy.

It learns how you write. Paste a writing sample in Settings and Breeze HR matches your tone, vocabulary and sign-off. It also tracks how your team edits its drafts. If you consistently shorten responses, remove ACAS references, or use a specific sign-off, it picks that up and adjusts automatically - without you having to ask.

It connects to your documents. Link SharePoint and Breeze HR can search your actual files - job descriptions, templates, contracts - and use them as a reference when creating new ones.

It is built for UK HR. It knows ACAS codes of practice, current statutory rates, employment tribunal risk, and the correct process for grievances, disciplinaries, redundancy and more. A general AI model has none of that context.
How does the learning model actually work?
Breeze HR learns from several signals that build up over time:

Every conversation. Every response Breeze HR generates is saved. Over time it analyses patterns - what topics your team asks about most, what language you use, what advice you act on.

How you edit drafts. When you click into a chat response and edit it before copying, Breeze HR compares what it wrote with what you actually used. It detects patterns: if you consistently make responses shorter, it becomes more concise. If you always change the sign-off, it adopts yours. If you remove certain phrases, it stops using them.

Ratings. Use the thumbs up / thumbs down buttons on any response. A thumbs up tells Breeze HR to write more like that. A thumbs down tells it to avoid that style entirely. These signals take effect from your very next message.

Your writing sample. Go to Settings and paste an example of how you write - an email, a letter, anything. Breeze HR matches your tone, vocabulary and phrasing across all drafts.

Explicit preferences. If you say "make it shorter", "less formal" or "use bullet points" in any conversation, Breeze HR picks that up and applies it going forward - it does not wait for you to say it again.
Does Breeze HR stay up to date with UK employment law?
Yes. Breeze HR is built on continuously updated AI that is trained on current ACAS guidance, UK employment legislation and statutory rates. Current rates built in include: National Living Wage (£12.21/hr for 21+), Statutory Sick Pay (£116.75/week), Statutory Maternity/Paternity Pay (£184.03/week or 90% of AWE), and statutory notice and redundancy pay rules.

You do not need to manually update anything. As the underlying model is updated with new legislation, rate changes or updated ACAS guidance, those changes flow through automatically. If a new right is introduced - such as changes to flexible working rules or carer's leave - Breeze HR will reflect it.
Your data and documents
Can I upload my own HR policies and company handbook?
Yes - and this is one of the most important things to do when you set up. Go to Policies and upload your documents as PDF, Word or text files. You can upload individual policies (annual leave, disciplinary, grievance etc.) as well as your full company handbook.

Once uploaded, Breeze HR reads everything and uses your documents first when answering questions. If an employee asks about your flexible working policy, it answers based on your specific policy - not a generic description of the law. If your policy is more generous than the statutory minimum, Breeze HR will say so.

If no policy exists for a topic, Breeze HR falls back to ACAS guidance and UK employment law and flags that you may want to create a policy for it.
Can I connect SharePoint and use my existing documents?
Yes. Connect SharePoint under Settings using your Microsoft account. Once connected, Breeze HR can search your SharePoint files directly from the chat.

The most powerful use case is document creation. If you ask Breeze HR to create a job description for a new role, it automatically searches your SharePoint for existing job descriptions, tells you what it found, and offers to use the same format and structure. The result is a new document that matches your company's existing style - not a generic template.

You can also search SharePoint manually at any time using the SharePoint panel (the icon in the sidebar), and add documents directly to a chat conversation.
Is each company's data kept completely separate?
Yes, completely. Every company has its own isolated workspace. Your policies, chat history, templates, responses and documents are never visible to other users or other companies on the platform.

Data separation is enforced at the database level using row-level security - every record is scoped to your company ID, so even at the infrastructure level your data cannot be accessed by anyone outside your organisation. User logins are managed through a secure authentication layer and each team member only sees their own chats and the shared company policies.
Documents and templates
What types of letters and documents can Breeze HR draft?
Over 25 built-in templates across contracts, letters and disciplinary documents:

Contracts: Standard employment contract, fixed term contract, internship agreement, contract variation, TUPE transfer letter.

Letters: Offer letter, promotion letter, salary increase, probation passed, flexible working approval and refusal, maternity and paternity confirmation, return to work, resignation acceptance, sickness absence review, settlement agreement, home working agreement.

Disciplinary and grievance: First written warning, final written warning, grievance acknowledgement, disciplinary hearing invite, dismissal letter, redundancy at-risk and confirmation letters, redundancy appeal outcome, performance improvement plan (PIP).

References: Standard employment reference, regulated reference (FCA/SYSC 22).

You can also upload your own Word or PDF templates. Mark variable fields with {curly brackets} or [square brackets] and Breeze HR detects them automatically, asks the right questions and fills the document in.
Can I upload my own contract or letter templates?
Yes. Go to Documents and click Upload template. Before uploading, mark any fields you want to fill in later using {curly brackets}, [square brackets] or <<angle brackets>> - for example {employee_name}, [Job Title] or <<start_date>>.

Breeze HR detects these automatically, creates a list of questions to fill each field, and builds the completed document live as you answer them. The final download is a Word file with your original formatting preserved.

This works with .docx, .pdf and .txt files. If your template uses a format like [PROBATION PERIOD, e.g. 3 months], Breeze HR strips the description and treats it as a fillable field.
Integrations and team access
Can I connect my email and manage HR correspondence in one place?
Yes. Connect Gmail or Outlook under Settings. Once connected, your inbox loads inside Breeze HR so you can read incoming emails, draft AI-assisted replies and send completed letters - all without leaving the platform.

Each team member connects their own personal email account, so emails remain private to them. Breeze HR can read the email you are responding to and use that context when drafting - so it knows who the employee is, what they asked and what the situation is before it writes a single word.
How do I get my whole HR team using Breeze HR?
Each team member creates their own account. Once you approve them (under Settings - you control who gets access), they log in and immediately have access to your company's uploaded policies, templates and handbook.

Their individual chat history, email connection and writing preferences are private to them. But the shared knowledge base - policies, templates, learning signals from edited drafts - builds up collectively. The more your team uses Breeze HR, the more it learns about how your specific company operates, and the better it gets for everyone on the team.
