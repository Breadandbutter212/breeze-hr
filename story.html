Our story

Why we built Breeze.

I spent eight years in people and HR - at INEOS, Argus Media and Perella Weinberg - and everywhere I went, the same thing held the work back: admin.

The strategic work, the part that actually helps people, kept losing to drafting letters, chasing approvals and updating trackers. So in 2025 I started building Breeze: an AI HR tool that takes the admin off your plate, learns how your team works, and lets HR spend its time where it matters - on people, not paperwork.
