Maternity leave confirmation template

Replace the highlighted fields. Confirm the leave end date within 28 days of notification.

[Date]

Dear [Employee name],

Confirmation of your maternity leave

Congratulations on your wonderful news. Thank you for letting us know. I am writing to confirm the details of your maternity leave.

Your maternity leave will begin on [leave start date]. Based on this, your leave can continue for up to 52 weeks, with an expected return date of [return date]. If you would like to return earlier, please give us at least 8 weeks' notice.

During your leave you will receive [Statutory Maternity Pay / enhanced maternity pay details]. [Briefly summarise the pay schedule.]

You can work up to 10 keeping-in-touch (KIT) days during your leave if you wish, without affecting your maternity pay. There is no obligation to do so.

Your role and terms are protected during your leave, and we very much look forward to welcoming you back. If anything changes or you have any questions, just let me know.

With best wishes,
[Your name]
[Your job title]

What to include

  • The confirmed leave start date and expected return date
  • Maternity pay details (SMP or enhanced)
  • How keeping-in-touch (KIT) days work
  • Reassurance that their role and terms are protected
  • Notice required if they want to return early

FAQs

How much maternity leave are employees entitled to?
Up to 52 weeks of statutory maternity leave - 26 weeks Ordinary and 26 weeks Additional - regardless of length of service.
When should you confirm the leave?
Confirm the leave end date in writing within 28 days of the employee notifying you of their intended start date.
