Questions

The honest answers

Is it really free right now?
Yes. While we're in early access it's free to use the full platform. We'd rather have you using it and telling us what works than charging before it's ready.
Will it stay free?
The platform won't be free for business forever - paid plans are coming. Teams who join now as design partners lock in preferential pricing when they launch.
What's the catch?
None. We ask for your honest feedback, that's it. No card required and nothing to cancel.
What happens to my data?
It stays yours. Your data is never used to train any AI model, it's EU-hosted, and each company is fully isolated. More on security.
