What is Breeze HR?

HR teams spend too much time answering the same questions, drafting the same emails and searching for the same policies. Breeze HR changes that.

Breeze HR is an AI-powered HR platform built for UK businesses. It connects directly to your Gmail or Outlook inbox, your SharePoint documents and your company policies - so when an employee emails about annual leave, a disciplinary or a pay query, Breeze HR drafts a compliant, policy-accurate reply in seconds. Not a generic template. Your policies, your tone, ready to send.

Beyond email, Breeze HR handles the documents HR teams produce every day - employment contracts, reference letters, settlement agreements, investigation reports. Generate them, edit them, download them as Word files or send them directly from the platform.

Everything is built around UK employment law, ACAS guidance and your own company policies. Breeze HR does not guess. It drafts based on what you have told it, flags what it does not know and keeps the HR professional in control at every step.

Built for HR professionals who belong in the room, not the inbox.
